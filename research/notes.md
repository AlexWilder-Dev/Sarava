# Sarava: research notes

Researched 18 October 2026. Sources are listed at the end. Anything not listed here was not found and stays off the site.

## Name, identity and the sign

- They trade as **SARAVA**. Their Google business description and X bio both say: *"The crepe artists formerly known as Senzala. In Brixton Village since 2010 serving French crepes with Brazilian flair."* Google adds: *"Steaks, salads, Amazing cocktails, beer and coffees."*
- **Logo (from the shopfront, photographed for their own Zideli booking page, uploaded July 2026):** a sun mark plus the wordmark. The mark is a semicircle of rays over a horizon line. Below the line, the sun's lower half is solid and three concentric half-rings ripple out under it, like a sun setting into water. The wordmark is "SARAVA" in a Futura-like geometric sans: all caps, pointed A apexes, generous tracking.
  - Fascia: red letters and mark on a teal-green painted fascia.
  - Side window: gold vinyl on black glass, stacked mark / SARAVA / "BRIXTON VILLAGE SINCE 2010".
  - Pillar menu poster: a blue-teal mark, "SARAVA", and "FRENCH CRÊPES WITH BRAZILIAN FLAIR".
- **Window signwriting:** white brush-script "GALLETES" (sic) and "COCKTAILS" over the windows, "HAPPY HOUR" on the door. A gold condensed board on the glass with a teal drop shadow reads: **"ALL COCKTAILS – £6 / 16:00–19:30 / Espresso Martini, Passion Fruit Caipirinha, Chili Margarita, Mojito, Piña Colada & more"**.
- I found no Instagram profile picture: Instagram returned 429 to every request. The logo is redrawn in SVG from the sign photo, and the wordmark is set in Jost to match the lettering.
- Founding year conflict: the sign and the Google/X bio say **2010**, while the Countertalk employer page says "stablished in 2009". I went with the sign.

## Where it is

- Unit 41–42, Granville Arcade, Brixton Village, 41–42 Coldharbour Lane, SW9 8PS. Google plus code FV6Q+X9.
- The official Brixton Village map (Mapme) puts 41–42 on **Second Avenue**, east side, between unit 40 and units 43–44, opposite 77–78. The "77-78" sign shows on the left of the shopfront photo, and a "2ND [AVEN]UE" sign sits above the fascia.
- Route: from Coldharbour Lane you enter through **Brixton Village Courtyard** and two avenues lead in. Take the **right-hand** one (Second Avenue) and pass 1·48·49, 47, 46, 45 and 43–44 on your right. Sarava is next on the right, with its teal fascia, red sign and tables out in the avenue. Fifth and Sixth Avenue branch off to the left.
- Brixton Village's own opening hours: Mon 8am–6pm, Tue–Sun 8am–midnight.

## The room

From the 2018 Loic Salan photos for Brixton Village and the 2026 shopfront photo:

- **Walls:** a saturated turquoise feature wall (about #04A9BE) and rough white-washed brick on the other side.
- **Art:** a dense salon hang of mismatched frames in gilt, dark wood, red and black. It mixes prints (a Renoir girl combing her hair, Michelangelo's *Creation of Adam*, a seascape, a cat, framed pages) with a red-lacquered box-frame piece with a pig/bear head and "VIVA … HERO" lettering. No artists are named anywhere.
- **Furniture:** mismatched wooden café chairs, some painted red, on chunky old wooden tables. Outside sit grey and white folding chairs.
- **Light:** caged ship's bulkhead pendant lamps, bare filament bulbs, and a big old chrome fan.
- **Small touches:** single flowers in glass bottles on each table, bananas hanging by the counter, and stacks of square white plates at the pass.
- **Outside:** the arcade has a glazed roof, a sky-blue painted upper wall and blue louvres. Paper lanterns hang across the avenue (Brixton Village's, not Sarava's). Tables run along the shopfront.
- **Character:** a small, family-run place (Countertalk). Google lists it as women-owned and LGBTQ+ friendly. A Google review (relayed via Nova Circle) names the owner as Lee. I left owner names off the site.
- **Community:** Sarava began with **The Brick Box**, an arts and community organisation (Brixton Village listing and old About page). The old site said "Our walls are a platform for artists to exhibit and sell their work". I can't tell whether that still runs. An "I SUPPORT #BUYBACKBRIXTON" poster is on the door.
- **Access (Google):** wheelchair-accessible entrance, parking, toilet and seating, plus an assistive hearing loop. Also outdoor seating, high chairs and vegan options.

## The food

- Every dish comes as a white-flour **crêpe** or a buckwheat **galette**. Time Out (2020) says the house suggestion is flour for sweet and buckwheat for savoury. Their own description (TripAdvisor/booknbook) says *"Our galettes are not just gluten-free but also lactose, egg and dairy-free and we have organic and vegan wines and beers."* The old printed menu says the crêpes contain gluten, wheat, egg, lactose and dairy. So vegan really means vegan filling on a buckwheat galette.
- **Hot sauce:** "Signature Chilly [sic] Sauce", £1.60 on Deliveroo under Homemade Sauces, alongside Garlic Butter £2.20 and Salad Dressing £1.40. An old blog says it's made from Brazilian chillies, pepper and lime with a secret recipe. That's unverified, so it isn't on the site.
- **Brazilian notes:** Churrasco (sirloin cap strips with cassava flour), biquinho ("little beak") peppers, plantain, black beans, Guaraná soda. Brixton Village also mentions "Brazilian-influenced tapas" and the Zideli page "tapas". There's also a Brixton Jerk galette.
- **Menu used:** the Deliveroo menu, headed "Sarava Creperie & Cafe" on a page still slugged senzala-creperie. Deliveroo itself is behind a Cloudflare check that this environment can't pass, so I read the page through the search engine's cached copy, section by section. Names, prices and (v)/(vegan) marks come from that copy. Most descriptions are verbatim; a few (Churrasco, Burrata Salad, Illunga Bread, drinks) were reconstructed from the cached text. These are **Deliveroo delivery prices**, and no dine-in menu was found: Google's "menu" link goes to Instagram, which is blocked, and the pillar poster in the photo is too small to read.
  - Starters: Chicken Caesar salad, Garlic Bread (v), Churrasco, Illunga Bread (vg), Burrata Salad.
  - Side salad: Mixed Leaves (vg). Main salad: Ribeiro Salad.
  - 21 savoury crêpes & galettes, from Complete (£11.40) to Gaucho Che (£17.20).
  - 13 sweet, from Real Canadian Maple Syrup (£5.60) to Banoffee and Apple d'Amour (£10.40).
  - Drinks: orange juice, homemade lemonade, Cokes, Guaraná soda, Dalston ginger beer, Pinkus lager, Cidre de Normandie, three wines.
  - The Burrata Crepe is tagged Vegetarian on Deliveroo but has Parma ham, so it carries no tag here.
- **Cocktails:** only the window list (above) is confirmed for 2026. An older 2013 menu had Rossini, Kir Royale, Bloody Mary and others, but that's too old to use.
- **Coffee:** reviewers mention coffee, chai latte, iced coffee, double macchiato and a "Lee-special" hot chocolate. No current coffee list or prices were found, so the site names coffee without listing it.

## How they talk

- Short, warm, plain, a bit cheeky, with exclamation marks. Their own lines:
  - "The crepe artists formerly known as Senzala."
  - "Food lovers, fussy eaters, gluten-free, vegetarians & vegans, all welcome!"
  - "French crepes with Brazilian flair."
  - "Steaks, salads, Amazing cocktails, beer and coffees."
- Zideli booking page: "Sarava is a creperie bar & cafe, offering crepes, tapas, cocktails and more" / "Be first to know about SARAVA's last-minute tables, special evenings and offers".
- The old menu dropped in Portuguese ("Bem vindo or welcome", "Food made with amor (love)", "É batata!") and Brazilian place-pride. I've kept that touch of Portuguese but none of the old Senzala imagery (Eiffel Tower, Christ the Redeemer, palms), which was generic anyway.
- They spell it "crepes" without the circumflex in their own copy, but the poster uses "CRÊPES". New copy on the site uses crêpe.

## Who comes when

- **Weekday daytime (café):** opens 10:30 Mon–Thu. Google popular times show late morning as the peak ("11 am: usually as busy as it gets, up to 15 min wait"). Reviewers describe quick weekday lunches in the Village, coffee and chai, gluten-free lunches, and families with children (high chairs).
- **Weekends (brunch):** opens 10:00 Fri–Sun. Reviews and the brief say Sunday brunch fills up and a brunch guide says to book bottomless ahead, but I found no current bottomless price, times or rules. The Brixton Village Mother's Day guide calls it "Brazilian Brunch with a Twist".
- **Evening (bar):** happy hour 16:00–19:30 with all cocktails £6. Open till 21:30 Tue–Thu and 22:00 Fri–Sat. Wanderlog says "good music playing in the evenings". Zideli booking types are Dinner, Drinks, Birthday party, Engagement party, Corporate event, Private hire and Christmas party, with a max party of 6 and tables held 15 minutes.

## What reviewers praise and complain about

- **Praise:**
  - Google 4.7 from about 860 reviews; TripAdvisor 4.6 from 303; Deliveroo 4.9 from 500+; Uber Eats 4.7 from 190.
  - Friendly, quick staff (one names Alessandro).
  - Big, filling portions and good value (about £20 a head).
  - Long menu with real vegan and gluten-free choice (London x London: "they cater really well for food intolerances and dietary requirements").
  - The hot sauce, the cocktails (especially at happy hour), and coffee, chai and mint lemonade.
  - The colourful room and people-watching in the Village. Time Out: "lively ambience, colourful decor, friendly staff and cosy interior help make it a Brixton Village staple".
- **Complaints (few):** stingy beef in one Picante, one sweet vegan crêpe "hard and dry", one "tense" dinner, and Time Out found some dishes weak (2020). Lunch is the quiet time if you want fast service.

## Design reasoning

- **Palette from the shopfront and room:**
  - Fascia teal #2E9C95 (deep #146B66)
  - Sign red #E5544B
  - Window black #1C1A17 with signwriter gold #D9B865
  - Turquoise feature wall #04A9BE
  - White-wash #F3EEE6
  - Chair red #8E1B22
  - Buckwheat brown #8B5A3C against crêpe gold #E9B97A, from the food photos
- **Type:** Jost (Futura-style geometric) set in tracked capitals for the wordmark and headings, matching the fascia letters, and also used for body text. Barlow Condensed in gold with a hard teal offset shadow reproduces the "ALL COCKTAILS – £6" window board for prices, times and live status.
- **Layout:** the page is built like their frontage. A teal **fascia** with red mark and wordmark is the header. Black **window** panels in gold signwriting carry live information (open now, happy hour). Real photos hang in mismatched frames like their salon wall rather than in a full-bleed hero. Phone first: the first screen gives the sign, what it is, open-now status and Book / Call / Directions.
- **Signature interactions, both from Sarava itself:**
  1. **Their sun mark sets.** The site runs in café mode by day and bar mode from happy hour (16:00), and the logo's sun sinks below its horizon into the ripples as it switches. Visitors can flip it by hand.
  2. **Crêpe ⇄ galette switch** on the menu. The drawn pancake turns from golden flour to speckled buckwheat, and the menu relabels the base "vegan & gluten-free". Vegan/vegetarian filters use only their (v)/(vg) marks, with a nudge onto buckwheat when vegan is picked.
  3. **Find us in the Village:** an SVG route from Coldharbour Lane through the Courtyard and up Second Avenue to 41–42, opposite 77–78, drawn from the official Brixton Village map.

## What the owners most want this site to do

1. Put the name on their sign online: someone searching "Sarava" should land on a Sarava site, not Senzala pages, an Instagram grid or a Deliveroo URL.
2. Turn interest into bookings without the phone: send tables to the Zideli system they already pay for, and catch parties, private hire and groups over 6 that Zideli can't take.
3. Show the menu, with buckwheat as the vegan and gluten-free base and the happy hour board, well enough that someone standing in the Village walks in now.
